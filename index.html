Featured Project

Exchange Simulator - Market Microstructure & Adverse Selection

TypeScript, React, Vitest, Seeded RNG
Real-time exchange simulator demonstrating why bid-ask spreads exist and how market makers adapt to toxic order flow. Features a price-time priority matching engine (same logic as real exchanges), an adaptive market maker that detects informed flow and widens spreads to survive, and configurable trader agents (informed vs. noise). Built with seeded RNG for deterministic, reproducible experiments. Visualizes the Glosten-Milgrom model of adverse selection.
